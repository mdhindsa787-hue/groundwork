Questions

Fair questions.

Does it sound like a robot?

Try the demo above and judge for yourself. It speaks naturally, waits for people to finish, and keeps things short and friendly.

Do I need a new number?

No. Your customers keep calling the number they already have. Calls you don't pick up roll over to the receptionist.

What about emergencies?

It flags urgent calls in the text so you see them first, and it tells callers you'll ring back as soon as you can. It never promises times or quotes prices.

What if I don't like it?

You don't pay anything until it's live on your phone and you're happy with it. After that it's month to month, cancel any time.
