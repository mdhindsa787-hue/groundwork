FAQ

Good questions.

Why did you build me a preview without asking?

It's the quickest way to show you what's possible. No obligation. If it's not for you, ignore it and it comes down.

What do you need from me?

Your best contact number, licence number if you have one, a few photos of your work, and anything you'd like to say about your business.

Are there ongoing fees on a website?

No lock-in. The domain is about $15–25 a year, registered in your name. Changes later are optional.

When do I pay?

Only after you've seen the finished site and you're happy with it. Bank transfer, invoice provided.

How does the AI receptionist cost work?

$149 a month with no lock-in contract. Your first payment is only once it's live on your phone and you're happy with it. Cancel any time.
